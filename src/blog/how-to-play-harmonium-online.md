## Playing Harmonium Online — It's Easier Than You Think

Thanks to modern web technology, you can now play harmonium without owning a physical instrument. Web Harmonium is a free, browser-based harmonium that works on any device with a keyboard.

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Open Web Harmonium

Simply navigate to [webharmonium.site](/) on your computer. No downloads, no installations — it works right in your browser.

### Step 2: Understand the Keyboard Layout

Web Harmonium maps your computer keyboard to harmonium notes:

- **White keys (lower row):** A, S, D, F, G, H, J, K, L map to the natural notes (Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni, Sa)
- **Black keys (upper row):** W, E, T, Y, U map to the sharp/flat notes
- **Extended range:** Q through P gives you a wider range of notes

### Step 3: Play Your First Note

Press any key on your keyboard. You should hear a harmonium note. Try pressing keys in sequence to play a simple scale:

> A → S → D → F → G → H → J → K

This plays the basic major scale: **Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni Sa**

## Features to Explore

### Reverb

Toggle the reverb effect to add depth and resonance to your sound. This simulates the natural echo of a concert hall.

### Transpose

Use the transpose control to shift all notes up or down. This is useful if you want to play in a different key without learning new finger positions.

### Octave Selection

Switch between octaves to play higher or lower notes. This gives you access to the full range of the harmonium.

### Reed Stacking

Enable reed stacking to layer multiple reeds, creating a richer, fuller sound — just like a real harmonium with multiple reed banks.

## Playing with a MIDI Controller

If you have a MIDI keyboard connected to your computer, Web Harmonium can use it as input:

1. Connect your MIDI controller via USB
2. Web Harmonium will automatically detect it
3. Play your MIDI keyboard — the harmonium sound will respond in real-time

This provides the most natural playing experience, as a MIDI keyboard has the same layout as a harmonium keyboard.

## Tips for Beginners

1. **Start with simple melodies** — Don't try to play complex ragas right away. Begin with familiar tunes
2. **Use one hand** — Unlike piano, harmonium is typically played with one hand while the other operates the bellows
3. **Practice scales** — Running through scales builds muscle memory
4. **Listen to recordings** — Listen to harmonium masters to develop your ear for the instrument's tone and expression

## Practice Anywhere, Anytime

One of the biggest advantages of an online harmonium is portability. You can practice at home, at school, during a break at work — anywhere you have a computer with internet access. Web Harmonium even works offline after the first load, so you don't need a constant connection.

Start playing today at [webharmonium.site](/) and discover the joy of harmonium music!
